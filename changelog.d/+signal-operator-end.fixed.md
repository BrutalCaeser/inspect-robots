**Rollout:** A supervisor can end an episode without waiting for the policy by sending `SIGUSR1` to `inspect-robots run` or `eval-set`. The signal interrupts a blocked policy call, discards any reply that arrives late, and ends the trial as an ordinary `operator_end` (scored, parked, logged), never as a cancellation. Motion already in flight finishes its step. A keypress End is still read between steps, so it first executes the reply the policy was waiting on.
